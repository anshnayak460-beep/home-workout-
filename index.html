⏱️ 20 Minute Workout Routine

1. Jumping Jacks — 2 Minutes

Start slowly and gradually increase your speed.

2. Push Ups — 3 Sets × 10

Keep your body straight while performing push-ups.

3. Squats — 3 Sets × 15

Keep your back straight and push through your feet.

4. Mountain Climbers — 2 Minutes

Maintain a steady pace and keep your core engaged.

5. Plank — 30 Seconds × 3

Keep your body in a straight line and breathe normally.

🌟 Fitness Tips

💧 Stay Hydrated

Drink enough water before, during and after your workout.

🥗 Eat Healthy

Include nutritious foods, vegetables, fruits and protein in your diet.

😴 Get Enough Sleep

Good sleep helps your body recover after exercise.

📅 Be Consistent

Regular workouts are more useful than exercising only occasionally.
